Still curious? Get in touch →
What programmes do you offer?

Our learning pathways include toddler, preschool, and pre-kindergarten programmes. Contact us to discuss programme details and which option may be the right fit for your child.

How can I enquire about enrollment?

You can share your programme of interest and a message using the enquiry form. Our team will be able to respond once the form has been connected to the centre’s enquiry system.

What are your opening hours?

Please get in touch for current opening hours, holiday closures, and schedule information.

How can I arrange a visit?

Send an enquiry and mention that you would like to arrange a visit. The team can then discuss suitable dates and the next steps.

How can I contact the centre?

Use the enquiry form to request contact information or ask a question. Direct email and telephone details will be added here once confirmed.
